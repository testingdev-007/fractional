    What the SATs ask about × and ÷ with fractions
    • Year 6 curriculum: multiply pairs of proper fractions (answer in simplest form) and divide a proper fraction by a whole number, like 1/3 ÷ 2 = 1/6.
    • Also tested from Year 5: multiply a proper fraction or a mixed number by a whole number, like 1 3/4 × 10.
    • Paper 1 (arithmetic) shows bare calculations, sometimes back to front (☐ = 2/3 × 3/5). Papers 2 and 3 (reasoning) put the same skills into word problems.
    • Marking: equivalent fractions, mixed or improper forms and exact decimals all score. Rounded decimals don't. This deck marks the same way, then shows the simplest form.
    • Known trouble spots: in 2019 only 43% of pupils got 5/6 × 540 right, and fewer than half got 1 3/4 × 10. Those question shapes appear often here.
    • Dividing by a fraction (like 3 ÷ 1/4) is not in the KS2 curriculum, so it lives in the optional Stretch cards.
